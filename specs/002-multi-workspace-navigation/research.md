# Phase 0 Research: Multi-View Clinical Navigation & Cognitive Ergonomics

## 1. Population Health Intake to Bedside Care Handoff
- In ACO REACH and Medicare Advantage programs, care managers screen cohorts of 200–500 patients every morning.
- The standard triage pattern:
  1. Filter by `Critical Alerts` (weight velocity $\ge +3.0\text{ lbs}$ in 48h OR resting HR drift $\ge +10\text{ bpm}$).
  2. Inspect patient row details (symptom survey, adherence).
  3. 1-Click transfer into the comprehensive patient workstation.
